## What we learned

- §5.7.3's variation and its increment fight each other at 500: the
  variation span at the touch is ±2,500 shares, so a 500 grid leaves
  only 11 landing spots and the "randomization" still reads as blocks.
  The grid, not the variation width, was the machine tell.
- The tell is not just granularity — it is ROUNDNESS. George's ruling:
  a book whose every size is a round lot reads as untouched (inactive);
  odd integers read as fills having happened. That killed the 100-grid
  first cut, not any technical defect.
- The §5.8 materiality threshold (500 sh) is a separate row from the
  grid. On the 500 grid every basis move was ≥ 500 and therefore
  material; ungridded, a sub-500 basis drift publishes nothing — the
  change can only LOWER publish cadence, never raise it.

## What we learned (addendum, the review)

- **A coarse grid can mask a determinism violation.** The 500-share
  rounding was silently absorbing a live-vs-replay difference in EPR
  for the whole life of the machine; the "replay equality green" line
  in the vault was true only because the difference never crossed a
  500 boundary in the tests. Any time a quantization is removed, re-run
  the equality proof against LIVE traffic, not just journals.
- **The same defect shape recurred at both ends of one round trip.**
  CA2's phase review fixed netting for PENDING_SUBMIT (registered, not
  yet in the book); the F5 review found the mirror — PENDING_REPLACE /
  PENDING_CANCEL (still in our record, already OUT of the book). Ask
  "is this IN the published book" of EVERY state, not just the one the
  incident showed.
- **Single-hop tests pass multi-hop bugs.** ANCHOR_SEED's every test and
  its acceptance criterion were one cutover deep; the defect is at two.
