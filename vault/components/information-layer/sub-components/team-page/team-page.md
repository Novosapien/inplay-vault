# InPlay Trading Challenge -- Team Page

> **Component:** [[information-layer]]
> **Status:** Collecting
> **Sources:** _[[meetings/08-05-2026-compoent-1]]_

---

Persistent team view. Historical data, season stats, matchup research. Enriched with live game data when the team is playing.

## Key Elements (from component session)

- Historical performance data (10-15 years from Sport Radar)
- Season stats and standings
- Head-to-head matchup data
- Stock price history for the team over time
- Live game data surfaced when the team is currently playing
- News feed items relevant to this team (injuries, signings, etc.)
- "Trade this team" CTA linking to order entry

## Open Questions

- Does the team page include player-level data, or only team-level?
- How deep does the historical data go in the UI? 10 years of data is a lot -- what's the default view?
- Does the team page show other users' trading activity on this team (e.g., "most traded team today")?
