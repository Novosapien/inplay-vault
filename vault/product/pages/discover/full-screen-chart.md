# Full-Screen Chart

> **Tab:** Discover
> **Purpose:** Expanded price chart for detailed analysis.
> **Map:** [[product/pages/PAGES|App Pages]]

---

## What Users See

A full-width, full-height candlestick chart that slides up as a modal overlay. For users who want to study price action in more detail than the inline charts on Game Page or Team Page allow.

---

## Key Elements

- **Candlestick Chart** — full-screen width, showing open/high/low/close candles
- **Time Range Selector** — switch between different time periods
- **Price Axis** — vertical scale with price labels
- **Team Identity** — team name and current price shown at top
- **Close Button** — dismiss the modal

---

## Where Users Go From Here

- Swipe down or tap close → returns to previous screen ([[product/pages/discover/single-game-page|Single Game Page]] or [[product/pages/discover/team-page|Team Page]])

---

## States

- **Game timeframe:** Shows intraday candles during a single game
- **Season timeframe:** Shows daily candles across the season
