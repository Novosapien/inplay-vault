# Settings

> **Tab:** More
> **Purpose:** Account preferences and app configuration.

---

## What Users See

Standard settings page for controlling notifications, appearance, and account details.

---

## Key Elements

- **Notifications** — toggle push notifications for:
  - Price alerts
  - Game events
  - Leaderboard movements
  - Referral activity

- **Appearance** — theme toggle (dark mode / light mode). Dark mode is the default for trading screens.

- **Account** — basic account information and management

- **Support** — help link / contact support

- **Sign Out** — log out of the app

---

## Where Users Go From Here

- Back → Settings Hub
- Sign Out → login screen

---

## States

Single state — always shows the full settings list with current values.
