# Referral Program

> **Tab:** More
> **Purpose:** Invite friends and earn InPlay dollars.

---

## What Users See

The referral program is a core growth mechanism. Users share their personal code; when someone signs up using it, both parties earn InPlay dollars for trading.

---

## Key Elements

- **Value Proposition** — "Get 1,000 / Give 500" — the referrer gets 1,000 InPlay dollars, the new user gets 500 InPlay dollars. Clear, simple, generous.

- **Personal Referral Code** — unique, lifetime-stable code for this user. Always visible and easy to copy.

- **Share Actions:**
  - Copy link button
  - System share sheet (text, WhatsApp, social media, etc.)
  - QR code generation (for in-person sharing, dot cards, t-shirts)

- **Referral Stats:**
  - Invites sent
  - Invites accepted (sign-ups)
  - Total earned from referrals

- **Referral History** — list of successful referrals with dates and amounts earned

---

## Where Users Go From Here

- Share actions → system share sheet / clipboard
- Back → Settings Hub

---

## States

- **No referrals yet:** Code and share buttons prominent, stats at zero, messaging encourages first share
- **Has referrals:** Stats populated, history shows successful conversions

---

## Why This Page Matters

Referrals are the primary organic growth driver. The "Get 1,000 / Give 500" is designed to be generous enough that users actively share — and the referral wallet is uncapped, meaning heavy referrers get significant trading capital advantage.
