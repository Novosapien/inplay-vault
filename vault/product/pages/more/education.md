# Education Hub

> **Tab:** More
> **Purpose:** Learn how the InPlay trading challenge works.
> **Map:** [[product/pages/PAGES|App Pages]]

---

## What Users See

Educational content for users who want to understand the mechanics of trading before risking their InPlay dollars. Particularly important for the "Sports-Passionate Casual" audience who may not have trading experience.

---

## Key Elements

- **Topic Cards** — structured learning modules:
  - **How Trading Works** — buy low, sell high; market orders vs. limit orders; what makes prices move
  - **Understanding Charts** — how to read candlestick charts, what price patterns mean, time ranges
  - **Risk Management** — position sizing, not putting all capital into one trade, when to cut losses
  - **Leaderboard Strategy** — how the three verticals work, how to optimise for different prize categories

- **Progress Indicators** — (future: track which modules have been completed)

## States

- **First visit:** All topics shown as unread, suggested starting order
- **Returning:** Progress tracked, completed topics marked

---

## Why This Page Matters

The app targets audiences with varying levels of trading literacy. The "Sports-Passionate Casual" audience understands sports deeply but may never have placed a trade. Education reduces the barrier to first trade and helps users make better decisions (which improves retention).
