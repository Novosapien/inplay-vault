# Dashboard (Home)

> **Tab:** Home
> **Purpose:** Personal overview — the first thing users see after opening the app.

---

## What Users See

The Dashboard answers three questions at a glance:

1. **How much do I have?** — Trading wallet and referral wallet balances displayed prominently at the top.

2. **How am I doing?** — Current ranking across all three competition verticals (Best P&L, Risk-Adjusted, Comeback), with a proximity indicator showing how far the user is from earning prizes (e.g., "112 places from earning").

3. **What's happening today?** — Today's game schedule with kickoff times, teams playing, and a summary of any positions the user holds in those games.

---

## Key Elements

- **Trading Wallet Balance** — the user's simulated trading capital (capped at 100,000 InPlay dollars)
- **Referral Wallet Balance** — earned from referrals (uncapped, separate from trading capital)
- **Ranking Cards** — one per competition vertical, showing current rank and movement direction
- **Proximity Indicator** — the gap between user's current position and the nearest earning threshold
- **Today's Games** — upcoming and live games with kickoff times
- **Active Positions P&L** — mini summary of unrealised profit/loss across all held positions

---

## Where Users Go From Here

- Tap a game → goes to Single Game Page (Discover tab)
- Tap a position → goes to Position Detail (Trade tab)
- Tap ranking card → goes to Leaderboard (Ranks tab)

---

## States

- **New user (no positions):** Wallet shows starting balance, no P&L section, games section encourages first trade
- **Active user:** Full view with positions, rankings, and upcoming games
- **No games today:** Games section shows next game day schedule
