# Position Detail

> **Tab:** Trade
> **Purpose:** Deep view of a single position in one team.
> **Map:** [[product/pages/PAGES|App Pages]]

---

## What Users See

Everything about the user's investment in one specific team — how much they hold, what they paid, how it's performing, and options to sell.

---

## Key Elements

- **Team Identity** — team name, colors, current stock price
- **Position Summary** — quantity held, average entry price, current value
- **Unrealised P&L** — profit or loss (dollar amount and percentage), color-coded
- **Trade History for This Team** — list of all buy/sell transactions for this specific team
- **Action Buttons** — Sell (partial or full position), Buy More

---

## Where Users Go From Here

- Tap Sell → [[product/pages/trading/confirmation|Trade Confirmation]]
- Tap Buy More → Order entry
- Back → [[product/pages/trading/portfolio|Portfolio]]

---

## States

- **Profitable position:** P&L shown in green, positive percentage
- **Losing position:** P&L shown in red, negative percentage
- **Single trade:** Simple history showing one entry
- **Multiple trades:** Full history showing averaging up/down over time
