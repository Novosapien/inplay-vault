# Trade History

> **Tab:** Trade
> **Purpose:** Complete record of all executed trades.
> **Map:** [[product/pages/PAGES|App Pages]]

---

## What Users See

A chronological log of every trade the user has completed — both buys and sells. This is the audit trail of all trading activity.

---

## Key Elements

- **Trade List** — each completed trade shows:
  - Team name
  - Direction (Bought or Sold)
  - Quantity
  - Execution price
  - Time and date
  - Realised P&L (for sells — how much profit or loss was locked in)

## States

- **Has trades:** Chronological list, most recent first
- **No trades yet:** Empty state encouraging first trade
