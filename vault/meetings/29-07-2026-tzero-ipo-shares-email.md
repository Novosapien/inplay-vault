## Post-Call Analysis

Pre-read email from **Evangelos Tzoulafis (tZERO, SVP Head of Project Execution)**, sent ahead of the call on **Thursday 30 July**. It confirms, from tZERO's side, the IPO shares-outstanding decision already captured in [[ipo-draft-requirements]] v2, and adds the rationale.

### What the email says

- **Production-like shares outstanding to match a $75m cap.** tZERO is moving shares outstanding to a production-like level chosen to match a **$75m cap**.
- **Shares:** **1,000,000 for NCAA** teams, **900,000 for NFL**. (Confirms the v2 numbers.)
- **Projected IPO prices attached for reference** = the model already stored in [[ipo-pricing-2026]] (no new file; the attachments are the pricing workbook and the requirements v2 doc already in the vault).
- **NCAA IPOs can run in parallel and stay open for 5 days.** (Confirms the v2 NCAA continuous multi-day, parallel offering.)
- **NFL IPOs:** tZERO wants to **simulate how it needs to work in production** (the sequential-window / water-line model in v2).

### Reading of the $75m cap

At the listed prices in [[ipo-pricing-2026]], the top NCAA name (Texas Tech ~$74.10) x 1,000,000 shares is ~$74m, and the top NFL name (LA Rams ~$81.20) x 900,000 shares is ~$73m. So the **$75m cap reads as a fully-diluted maximum market-cap ceiling** that the shares-outstanding were sized against, given the pricing. The exact definition (per-team fully-diluted cap? something else?) should be confirmed on the 30 July call. Logged in [[open-questions]].

### Net

This is tZERO confirming the v2 shares decision and the NCAA parallel / 5-day approach, and signalling that the NFL offering should mirror production mechanics. No change to the numbers already in [[ipo-draft-requirements]]; it adds the $75m-cap rationale and tZERO's sign-off.
