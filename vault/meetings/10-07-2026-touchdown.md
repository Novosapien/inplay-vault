### **Decisions**

## Needs Further Discussion

* **Community feature implementation deferred** The team will postpone the discussion regarding the implementation of a dedicated in-app community tab until next week due to cost and technical complexity.

## Aligned

* **Broker-dealer compliance structure established** The team will implement a "Chinese wall" between the trading operations and advisory side to enable the distribution of financial recommendations without requiring a specific broker-dealer license.

* **Influencer analyst strategy confirmed** The team will hire four to five influencer analysts in the sports space to generate and distribute their own fair value pricing within the app.

* **Media partner acquisition prioritized** The team will pursue media partnerships within the sports realm to distribute pricing and drive content reach.

* **UI line styling standardized** The team will standardize the UI lines on the cards by ensuring consistent color and thickness across all teams.

* **Forward-looking data display mandated** The app will zero out historical records at the top of the team pages to maintain consistency with the forward-looking IPO strategy.

* **IPO pricing countdown marketing strategy** The team will implement a public countdown for IPO prices to build momentum and drive user signups.

* **Synthetic market maker inventory plan** The team will utilize a synthetic market maker to manage inventory and ensure distribution for the initial launch simulation.

* **Discord integration for community engagement** The team will explore pointing users to a Discord server to provide a community space while avoiding the high costs and moderation complexities of in-app chat.
