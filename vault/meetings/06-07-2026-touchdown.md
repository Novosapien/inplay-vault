### **Summary**

The team reviewed app development progress and educational content strategy while confirming the postponement of chat features.

**App Performance and Metrics**  
The team reviewed initial user acquisition metrics and identified registration friction points. UI and UX designs were finalized to ensure readiness for the upcoming launch.

**Technical and Content Refinements**  
Standardization of team statistics and NFL branding was established to improve visual accuracy. Strategies for optimizing dense educational materials to boost engagement were analyzed.

**Launch Strategy Decisions**  
The team officially postponed the in-app chat feature to focus resources on core functionality. Proposed streak mechanics offer a potential pathway to improve retention before the trading challenge.

### **Decisions**

## Needs Further Discussion

* **Review of education module length** The team will further evaluate the optimal length and content format for education modules following concerns about user attention spans.

## Disagreements

* **Restricted access to TestFlight builds** Requests to provide external partners access to TestFlight builds were declined due to concerns over intellectual property security.

## Aligned

* **Nudge functionality for pending referrals** The team will add a nudge feature to the referral section to allow referrers to poke pending sign-ups.

* **Static data approach for IPO page** The team will adopt a static data approach for team IPO pages, involving manual criteria setting and verification for player upsides and downsides.

* **Ad-free Friday app release** The upcoming Friday app release will launch without ads to prioritize timely delivery and technical stability.

* **Referral links directed to App Store** Referral links will be redirected to the App Store rather than the current trading website once the app is live.

* **Implementation of user streak mechanic** The team will implement a daily streak mechanic to drive user retention and app engagement.

## Shelved

* **Chat feature deferred for launch** Development of the in-app chat feature is deferred indefinitely until user activity thresholds are met to justify the infrastructure costs.
