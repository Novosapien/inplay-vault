## Post-Call Analysis

The working session with Edwin Johnson and Cody Haugen that produced the advertising outbound onboarding pack. Present: Edwin, Cody, Brett and Max.

**This transcript is the source record for documents that already exist.** The session output was authored into the vault on 12 August as [[offer]], [[icps]] and [[buyer-personas]], with [[advertising/advertising]] as the section hub. Those documents carry the substance, their own gaps lists, and the four blocking items that prevent them being loaded into the Cold Outreach Workforce. Nothing is re-extracted here.

What the session settled is recorded in full at [[advertising/advertising]]: the offer routes as media with two component tracks; title sponsorship collapses into the sponsorship track rather than standing alone; volatility moments fill programmatically because there is more inventory than direct sales can absorb; 2,200 games replaces the old 2,116 count; betting, daily fantasy, sportsbook and casino are declined outright on every path; the audience is 18 to 55 with no gender split claimed; and no audience number goes in front of an advertiser, with sponsorship committing an exposure quantity instead.

Three facts surfaced that appeared in no earlier document: the **simulation multiplier**, where the back-test and strategy labs replay games so inventory is not bounded by the live-game count and which is currently unmodelled; that **title sponsorship reaches outside the app** into InPlay's own outreach and landing pages, which nothing in the mechanism produces and nobody owns; and the **Omnicom position**, where the $60M expectation is not happening while the Omnicom and WPP relationships remain live at $150K for a point of view and $250K to $500K for a full campaign, paused pending audience evidence.

Filed as the traceable source. Processing of the outbound campaign itself is deliberately on hold.
