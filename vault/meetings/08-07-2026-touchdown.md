### **Summary**

Meeting discussions focused on visual branding updates and advertising rollouts alongside critical operational and financial model adjustments.

**Brand and Visual Refinement**  
The team agreed to transition from gradient designs to flat colors with borders for accurate representation. They also decided to align investor deck visuals with the application palette.

**Operations and Compliance**  
Development priorities were set for the research module and store submissions. The team confirmed that trading in the application is strictly simulated to ensure regulatory compliance.

**Financial and Technical Updates**  
Financial models were corrected for seasonal variance using bottom-up data. The team also determined education modules require restructuring into shorter, more engaging segments.

### **Decisions**

## Needs Further Discussion

* **Trading competition eligibility criteria** The team is determining the eligibility requirements for the trading competition, specifically concerning tax ID and visa restrictions for international participants.

* **App navigation swiping functionality** The team is evaluating a proposal to switch from vertical scrolling to swiping functionality for navigation within specific app sections.

## Aligned

* **House ad implementation strategy** The team decided to implement house advertisements as placeholders within the app to demonstrate potential ad space for future marketing partners.

* **Education module duration limit** The team decided to standardize the education modules to be one to two-minute clips to improve engagement.

* **Team color display format** The team decided to remove gradients on team tiles and instead utilize flat primary colors with secondary color borders to ensure accurate color representation.

* **Historical data notation standardization** The team decided to add explicit notation to all historical data views to clearly differentiate them from projected 2025 season data.

* **Empty data field display** The team decided to display consistent 'No injuries reported' text in empty data fields rather than removing the interface elements, to maintain UI uniformity.
