Brand Guidelines

Logo Usage

Keep the InPlay mark clear, legible and consistent across every surface.

Do
  • Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the mark.
  • Use the transparent PNG on photography.
  • Use the primary orange on dark navy backgrounds.
Don't
  • Stretch, rotate or recolour the mark.
  • Place the logo on low-contrast imagery.
  • Add drop shadows or outlines.
