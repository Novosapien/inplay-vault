How Novosapien works the core platform from here

The core platform is delivered, so the core engagement moves to best effort. Everything the July proposal committed for launch is built and in users' hands, the market is open, and the engines are running unattended. From this point Novosapien's work on the core platform, meaning trading, the market maker and taker, the offering engine, the data platform, onboarding and the app itself, is provided on a best-effort basis: defects are fixed, the weekend is covered, and the engines are watched, but there is no longer a queue of committed launch scope behind it.

What best effort means in practice, stated plainly so it is not read as either a promise or a withdrawal.

  • Defects get fixed and are not queued behind new work. Anything that stops a user trading is worked immediately, as the buying-power problem is being worked now.
  • Live game days are covered. At least one Novosapien engineer is on standby for every game weekend, starting with this one.
  • There is no committed response time, because there is no support agreement in place that would set one. That is the gap described below, and it is the reason the support programme was submitted.
  • New work is still triaged and slotted, on the quarter pages, with its displacement named. Best effort on the core does not mean the roadmap stops; it means the core is no longer the thing consuming the capacity.
  • Committed work is still committed. The NFL offering on 5 September, the pricing model, the earnings report cycle and season-end settlement are Priority 1 commitments and are unaffected by this.

⚠ Support and maintenance: there is no cover in place, and this needs a decision

Recorded here because it is the largest unmanaged operational risk in the programme, and it is not a build item. The platform is now live, real users are trading, and there is no support contract, no support rota, no ticketing route and no agreed response time. What has covered it so far is the build team answering as things arise, which works while the build team is at full strength and stops working the moment it is not. This was first flagged on 29 July as the one thing missing from InPlay's coverage, and it has not moved since.

The friends and family game · overnight, 21 August

A live session with Edwin, Troy and the InPlay team on the app, the night before the offering. Findings below are taken from the session's own message log. The value of the night is that it produced exactly one class of problem that would have ruined the 22nd, and produced it in front of six people rather than six thousand.

What happenedDetailState

Closed: every finding from the night is fixed, and the one that mattered was fixed before the window opened. Three of the findings above are the same problem seen from different angles. The maker now quotes one level, so the book is thin by design. A market order into a thin book either fills what it can and cancels the rest, or it is refused. Last night it was refused, repeatedly, at every price. The server already has a residual sweep that cancels the unfilled remainder of a synthetic order, so the behaviour exists; what is not yet established is why the venue refused these sells outright. The most likely explanation, and it needs confirming from the rejection text rather than assuming, is the venue's own rule that a sell cannot exceed what the account can prove it holds. That is the same rule requests 13 and 14 ask to have removed, and Edwin has already raised it with Troy. It was settled the same week: the sell path was made forgiving, and the book was given its depth back. This is what the session was for. Six people found on the Thursday night what six thousand would otherwise have found on the Saturday.
The trade-off, and how it resolved. Wider and thinner quoting was asked for so that prices move with the market rather than sitting pinned to the win probability. The cost showed up the same night: less depth means more rejected exits. The recommendation was to keep the wider spread and make the exit path forgiving rather than thicken the book and lose the price movement. That is what was done, and then a second correction followed: one rung turned out to be too few for the book to repair itself, so it went back to three while keeping the wider spread. Both halves of what Edwin wanted are now in place.

How to read the sizing and the confidence

Size uses the same frame as the quarter slots: large is a multi-day build with real unknowns, medium is a day or two of focused work, small is hours. Size is the build, not the wait: an item can be small and still take a fortnight if it is queued behind someone else.

Confidence is where the honesty lives. High means Novosapien has built the like of it before and the unknowns are named. Medium means the shape is clear but the refinement is not. Low means the estimate covers something not yet opened, and the number will move. Standing up a feature is hours; making it dependable is where the time goes, and confidence tracks that second half, not the first.

Blocked items carry no confidence rating, because the estimate is meaningless until the dependency clears. They are listed with the name of whoever holds them.
