How to read the sizing and the confidence

Size uses the same frame as the quarter slots: large is a multi-day build with real unknowns, medium is a day or two of focused work, small is hours. Size is the build, not the wait: an item can be small and still take a fortnight if it is queued behind someone else.

Confidence is where the honesty lives. High means we have built the like of it before and the unknowns are named. Medium means the shape is clear but the refinement is not. Low means we are estimating something we have not yet opened, and the number will move. Standing up a feature is hours; making it dependable is where the time goes, and confidence tracks that second half, not the first.

Blocked items carry no confidence rating, because the estimate is meaningless until the dependency clears. They are listed with the name of whoever holds them.
