## Why this exists

The [[delivery|flight plan]] is client facing and carries delivery status, not
commercial terms. That is the right split, but it leaves a gap: things that were
said on a call, that carry money, and that nobody is tracking. This register is
the internal place for those. **Nothing here goes in the flight plan.**

**The rule.** An item appears here when it (a) has a money value or implies one,
(b) was agreed or promised outside a signed instrument, and (c) has no owner
chasing it. Each row cites where it was said, verbatim where a quote exists, so
the conversation can be reopened on the record rather than on memory.

## C1 · The launch bonus

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | 🟡 **Promised, undefined, unclaimed** |
| **Promised by** | Edwin Johnson |
| **Promised to** | The Novosapien team |
| **When** | Wednesday 15 July 2026, [[15-07-2026-touchdown]], at roughly 00:08:34 |
| **Amount** | Not stated |
| **Trigger** | "our launch". Not defined further |
| **Instrument** | None. Verbal only |

**What was said, verbatim.**

> **Edwin:** _"it's probably going to be a little bit tough. Okay. And like, you
> know, we launch, you know, what I plan on doing um for the Novo team, you know,
> we have an agreement. **We still don't have an agreement, but we have an
> agreement. So, the monthlies that I pay, um, you know, I'm going to allocate an
> additional bonus for our launch, okay? Um, because I want the team to know how
> much we appreciate. We couldn't be here without you. We need you.** Um, but the
> next five or six weeks are going to be f\*\*\*\*\*\* rough. I mean, they just
> are. And you know I I want to be very clear that it's You know, I can't not
> have you. All right?"_

**How to read it fairly.** He flagged the ambiguity himself in the first line.
This was said in the same breath as warning that the next five or six weeks would
be hard, and it reads as a retention and appreciation gesture ahead of a hard run
rather than a negotiated commercial term. Nobody on either side has referred to
it since.

**What is undefined, and all of it is:**

- **The amount.** "An additional bonus" against "the monthlies that I pay". No
  figure, no basis, no percentage.
- **The trigger.** "Our launch" was, on 15 July, a single future event. There
  have since been three defensible readings of it: the app going live in the
  stores, the NCAA offering opening on 22 August, or the NFL offering and season
  kickoff in September. **The trigger has arguably been met at least twice.**
- **The recipient.** "The Novo team" rather than Novosapien the company. Whether
  it is a payment to the company or a distribution to named individuals changes
  the tax treatment on both sides.
- **The mechanism.** Nothing says whether it rides an invoice, a separate
  payment, or an adjustment to a monthly retainer.

**Where it is recorded.** Three places, all inside the same file, and all of
them treat it as background rather than as an item: the 15-07 summary line, the
commercial row of the findings table (marked _"Parked — commercial"_), and the
transcript itself. It appears in **no** proposal, **no** invoice, **no** flight
plan and **no** other meeting.

**⚠ The exposure.** A promise this warm and this vague decays. It was made when
launch was a risk to be survived; the longer it goes unraised after launch has
succeeded, the more it reads as a nice thing someone once said. **The evidence
position will never be stronger than it is now:** the market opened on the date
the July proposal named, the NCAA secondary is live, and the delivered-versus-
planned count stands at 217% of committed launch scope on 90% of the planned
time.

**What would close this row.** Any one of: a stated figure, a written decline, or
a decision by Brett not to pursue it. All three are acceptable outcomes; leaving
it unraised is not.

**Owner:** Brett. **Deliberately not in the flight plan**, because a bonus line in
a delivery document handed to the client reads as an invoice and would undercut
the delivered-versus-planned card that is doing the real work.

## Maintaining it

Add a row when money is promised, waived, disputed or implied outside a signed
instrument, at the point it is said rather than later, with the citation. If an
item cannot be cited to a transcript, a document or an email, say so in the row
rather than leaving the source blank.
