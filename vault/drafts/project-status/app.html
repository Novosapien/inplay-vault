Consumer app IN BUILD — LATE STAGE

"InPlay Challenge" — iOS live, web build available, Android listing in progress

Where it stands

The app is in the store and receiving weekly improvement at high tempo — over 300 commits in July alone, delivered through an over-the-air update pipeline that avoids repeated store review. All five tabs are functional. The one big substitution still to come: market prices are simulated until the trading service connects, behind a kill switch that shows "TBA" app-wide if needed.
