IPO draft module IN BUILD

How every tradable asset comes into existence — the gating event for the season

Where it stands

The IPO Draft Requirements v2 (28 July) is the adopted baseline. Scope: 138 NCAA team companies (1,000,000 shares each) in a five-day continuous offering from 22 August, and 32 NFL team companies (900,000 shares each) in 18 one-minute sequential rounds across 5–6 September. The draft screens (swipe and list browsing, team detail, buy flow) are built in the app; the offering engine is the build focus alongside trading.

Design settled

  • Buy-only during the offering: no selling, no shorting; InPlay Markets is the exclusive seller.
  • NFL rounds: 50,000 shares per team per round; a pre-buy book of binding orders fills instantly at window open; per-participant cap of 2,500 shares per team per round to spread ownership.
  • Water-line load balancing keeps float roughly equal across teams without alphabetical-order artifacts; round order alternates alphabetical / reverse.
  • The market maker sweeps every unfilled share in rounds 1–10 (the primary mandate), so every team reaches the secondary market tradable; unsold inventory seeds the market maker's opening book.
  • Oversubscribed pre-buy books allocate pro-rata, residuals by random draw — an explicit fairness choice over first-come racing.
  • Shares-remaining hidden until an offering nears close (Edwin, 17 June).
  • NCAA IPO prices pushed into the app (24 July); NFL off-field values already baked into NFL listing prices.

In flight

  • IPO countdown and announcement mechanics across push, social and CRM.

  • Expected-wins model ownership: Edwin supplies the projections; the weekly refresh process is being formalised.
