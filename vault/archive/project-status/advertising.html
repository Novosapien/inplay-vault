Advertising IN BUILD

Edwin's stated number two priority — two motions: direct sponsorships and always-on programmatic

Where it stands

The ad stack is decided and moving: AppLovin MAX as ad server and mediator (confirmed 17 July), with a curated portfolio of SSP exchanges plugged in as adapters and AdMob loaded first. House ads run in the app from day one so users never experience a no-ads-to-ads flip. With the App Store listing now live, SSP account onboarding — which requires a real store listing — is unblocked.

Delivered

  • The programmatic media playbook: ranked SSP roster (8–12 target exchanges; AppLovin MAX, AdMob, Liftoff, PubMatic as day-one anchors), auction architecture, onboarding timelines, and weekly KPI targets.
  • Operating model: one human plus an AI agent workforce for ad ops (yield optimisation, anomaly detection, fraud, reconciliation, forecasting) in place of a conventional multi-person team.
  • The media-plan forecast calculator — a bottom-up page × persona revenue model with fill-rate, CPM and audience-uplift assumptions — built and reviewed with Edwin and Cody; PowerPoint versions (moderate and high growth) supplied for Edwin's investor conversations.
  • House ad units designed and shipping: the "What is InPlay?" hype-video unit with referral link, and education re-entry ads.
  • Kevel formally on hold until the first direct deal (1–2 week setup when triggered); a Kevel-ready abstraction is built in so the switch needs no rework.
  • Watch Mode ad surfaces designed: field-overlay logos, stoppage-only video slots, presented-by chart lockups (~720 impressions per game targeted).

In flight

  • SSP registration — proceeding now the store listing exists; exchanges verify live apps with volume.
  • Measurement stack — Google Tag Manager setup and the mobile-measurement-partner choice (Kochava vs AppsFlyer) in evaluation.
  • Constraint absorbed — AdMob compliance enforces a 30-second minimum ad refresh; rotation plans adjusted accordingly (start at 20s where policy allows, measure, then tune).
  • Direct-sale pilot construct ready ($50k–$250k over 2–4 weeks with an earn-out guarantee); two-pager with Omnicom's contact.
  • Complementary-advertisers-only rule in force to protect click-through rates.

Understandings for the first season

  • The playbook's own framing: the first 3–6 months are calibration, not optimisation — fill rates and eCPM curves stabilise only after ~30 days of live traffic and a season cycle.
  • Launch expectations are set conservatively (fill ~30% rising toward 80%+ mature; blended CPM treated as a floor).
  • Open modelling questions: the impression model's audience mix, ad-ops cost versus margin, and the advertiser KPI framework (CPA/CPM vs engagement metrics) — tracked for resolution with real launch data.

Next

  • Open the anchor SSP accounts against the live listing; run house ads until fill arrives.
  • Stand up GTM + the chosen MMP before launch campaigns.
  • Configure Kevel when the first direct deal signs.
