Third space — community DEFERRED

The social layer: banter during games, team communities, and conversational research

Where it stands

In-app chat is deferred until user activity justifies the infrastructure cost — agreed 6 July. The full design exists and is ready to activate; the open question is the interim: on 10 July the team agreed to evaluate a Discord community as the cheaper way to give users a space without the moderation burden. That evaluation has not yet been closed.

What is designed and ready

PieceDesign
Game Day ChatOne room per matchup, two fanbases together; the room ends when the game ends
Team / Favourites ChatPersistent, per-team, forum-style community
Research AI ChatPrivate natural-language questions over the Sportradar dataset — this thread now lives on in the Research module ladder
ModerationUser-reported and AI-assisted, with community moderators; InPlay does not staff moderation. Profanity tolerated on an age-verified platform, hate speech not; warnings before bans
InfrastructureBuild-vs-buy settled in principle: an open-source headless chat platform, with InPlay building the lifecycle glue, moderation and admin surfaces

Open

  • The Discord decision — adopt as the interim community space, or wait for in-app chat. Terms-of-service check was assigned; no closure recorded.
  • Activation threshold — "user activity justifies the cost" has no number attached yet.
  • Platform selection, community-moderator role definition, and membership rules — all parked with the deferral.
  • Future state (unchanged): sentiment and thematic data packaged for advertisers; creator-owned channels.

Next

  • Close the Discord evaluation — it is a cheap, fast interim win if adopted.
  • Define the activation threshold so the deferral has a trigger, not just a direction.
