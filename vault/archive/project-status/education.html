Education IN BUILD — BEGINNER TIER LIVE

The on-ramp for people who have never traded — and a store-review requirement

Where it stands

The 16 refreshed beginner modules are live in the app (since 17 July), every video regenerated under the agreed 2-minute cap with narration timed to measured audio. The full course is produced: 36 videos across beginner, intermediate and expert tiers, built on a consistent design system with AI voiceover. Card library format: watch or read, take a 2–3 question quiz, earn 100 InPlay coins into the referral wallet.

Delivered

  • Format reset (22 June): TikTok-style reels dropped for launch in favour of the card library — simpler, faster to ship, easier to sponsor.
  • All 36 module videos rendered; 16 beginner modules re-cut sub-2-minutes and shipped 17 July.
  • Kevin's three source guides (beginner, intermediate, expert — ~5,600 lines of source content) committed as the content base.
  • Glossary, non-sequential module access, earn-once rewards and greyed-completed states agreed and designed.
  • Success metrics proposed: 60% of new users open Education in their first session; 40% complete at least one module.

In flight

  • Module re-slice — Troy and Kevin are re-cutting the source documents into smaller subtopics (expecting 3–4× the module count); regeneration follows the revised documents.
  • Content review pass — Troy's watch-through flagged 5–7 areas for rework (ordering of concepts, one script rewrite); Jared's review flagged filler panels to cut. Post-launch drop-off metrics will drive further cuts.
  • Intermediate and expert tiers — rendered but still on estimated narration timings; they get the same refresh treatment as beginner.
  • Open decisions: quiz-failure handling (retry policy), whether KYC-pending users accrue rewards, flat vs tiered reward amounts, and who signs off content accuracy before publish.

Phased later

  • Sponsor slide-group ownership and the sponsor portal (manual placement first).
  • The education AI chatbot (phase 2).
  • Short-form / social-cut versions — deferred until post-launch metrics prove appetite.

Next

  • Receive the re-sliced source documents; regenerate the affected modules.
  • Apply the sub-2-minute refresh to intermediate and expert tiers.
  • Wire education completion into the prize-qualification criteria once those are finalised.
