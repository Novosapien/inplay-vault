Withdrawals & payouts NOT STARTED — NEEDS A SESSION

Converting winnings into real money — the least-specified launch-critical surface

Where it stands

This is the honest gap in the programme. The component is a stub: the design intent is agreed, but no build has started, the payment provider is unsigned, and a dedicated specification session with InPlay has not yet happened. The 24 July ranking was explicit — no trading would be a far worse launch outcome than no payout capability — so payouts sit in the launch remainder, with an agreed safety net.

What is agreed

  • Capture at withdrawal, not signup — bank details, crypto wallet address and US tax (1099) information are collected at the first withdrawal request, keeping signup friction-free. A deliberate, repeated decision.
  • Cash wallet lives on the tZERO chain, not on InPlay infrastructure — avoiding store-of-value licensing exposure.
  • The safety net (Edwin, 24 July) — if the provider is not signed in time, users always see the amount they are owed in the app, and payment runs on an interim manual rail (Zelle or wire) with a delay of up to a couple of weeks.
  • Eligibility is checked against the referral component's rules with a transparent checklist — requirements are shown, never hidden.

Open — the list is long, and known

  • Payment provider — unsigned; several candidates from the June conference; Edwin owns the relationship.
  • Eligibility ruling — international students and visa holders without a US tax ID currently fail verification; whether cash prizes are limited to US persons with a tax ID is with counsel. Flagged urgent 8 July.
  • The withdrawal UX itself: minimum threshold, frequency limits, holding periods, KYC re-verification, FX handling, failure modes, fraud review, support routing — eleven recorded unknowns awaiting the dedicated session.
  • Cash-wallet host confirmation with tZERO (stablecoin mechanics per the May integration call).
  • Tax-form generation (1099) process and ownership.

Next

  • Book the dedicated withdrawal session with InPlay — the single action that unblocks specification.
  • Provider signature → integration build; until then, stand up the owed-amounts display and the manual-rail process.
  • Get the eligibility ruling before the first prize cycle pays out.
