Earnings report SPECIFIED — BUILD SCHEDULED

The weekly tradable event that keeps every stock alive between games

Where it stands

This is the most fully specified unbuilt component: all five sub-systems are decomposed with entity journeys and acceptance criteria. Build is deliberately sequenced after the IPO and trading path — the first report cycle is needed in the opening weeks of the season (Tuesday NFL, Wednesday NCAA, released ~7:30 in a Bloomberg-style batch), not on IPO day.

Design settled

  • Every team company publishes a weekly off-field earnings figure: an Estimate the week before, an Actual on the day — the gap re-prices the stock.
  • Off-field pool: $250 per game, allocated by each team's share of matchup trade volume; the off-field total per game is half the on-field winner's earnings.
  • Corner cases ruled by Edwin in July: bye weeks earn no allocation; when a tradable team plays a school with no offering (~180 of ~2,100 games), 100% of the pool goes to the tradable team.
  • Estimates are designed to be tradable — built from attendance, jersey sales and viewership with deliberate spread (Dallas ≈ $30 vs Carolina ≈ $14) — and the same projection feeds the market maker's reference price.
  • Surfaces: a batched release feed with favourites and portfolio pinned to top, graphical report cards with an embedded trade button, a per-team history archive, a coloured earnings dot on the price chart, and a countdown with a push alert ~15 minutes before release.
  • Two routes only: a dedicated page under More, plus the reports box on each team page — each with a trade button on the report itself.

Open

  • Zero/negligible-volume allocation rule for the $250 pool.
  • Estimate methodology ownership and its weekly production process (pairs with Edwin's Wednesday data drop).
  • Anti-manipulation design — coordinated volume inflation to engineer a beat is identified as the core exploit loop and not yet designed against.
  • Release-burst load handling for the synchronised 7:30 spike.
  • Visual design — direction is set ("Bloomberg energy, not Bloomberg density") but no mockups exist yet.

Next

  • Build the earnings engine first (the proprietary core — the feed and cards have nothing to render without it), starting as the trading path lands (~week of 31 August).
  • First live cycle in the opening weeks of the season; the history archive and chart dot can fast-follow.
