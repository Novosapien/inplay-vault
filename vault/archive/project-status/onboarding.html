Customer onboarding & KYC LIVE

From "I just heard about InPlay" to logged in and ready to trade

Where it stands

The app is live on the Apple App Store as "InPlay Challenge" (since ~22 July), with over-the-air updates delivering improvements without store review. Onboarding runs end to end in production: create account → email verification → Persona identity check → land on the IPO page.
  • 83 approved KYCs as of 24 July (up from 64 the week before); 37 first-time downloads on 22 July.
  • Persona identity verification is contracted and performing: roughly 99.5% automated, face-scan callback in 2–3 seconds.
  • Signup captures email and password only — every other identity field comes from the government document. Three taps or fewer to start trading remains the design rule.
  • Users in the holding state get full browse access with trading buttons greyed out, never hidden.

Delivered

  • Onboarding flow locked (17 June) and built into the app; demoed 10 June, iterated through July.
  • Persona contract signed and integrated (early June).
  • Wallet allocation moved off the signup critical path — wallets can be provisioned the day before trading starts.
  • Referral popup and code issuance wired to KYC completion.
  • Apple review objection (age verification) answered and cleared; app approved and live.

In flight

  • Google Play listing — screenshots and a frozen build in preparation; the submission is framed to avoid a gambling classification, which would trigger much heavier review. Apple is expected to carry 70–80% of users.
  • KYC drop-off nudges — users who abandon mid-KYC (common at crowded campus signups) get a reminder push notification.
  • Eligibility question — international students and visa holders without a US tax ID fail verification; whether participation is limited to US persons with a tax ID is with InPlay and counsel.
  • KYC-failure handling (retry policy, soft vs hard fail, support route) and the Persona-outage fallback flow are still to be defined.

Next

  • Complete the Google Play listing.
  • Ship the KYC nudge notifications.
  • Confirm the eligibility ruling with counsel before prize payouts begin.
