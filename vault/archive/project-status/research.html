Research module IN BUILD — V1 DEMOED

The subscription product: Bloomberg-grade research over the Sportradar dataset, with a trade button on every report

Where it stands

Version 1 — pre-canned weekly reports with sortable columns and click-in definitions — was demoed live in the app on 13 July. Edwin asked (24 July) for the subscription piece to land in the next weeks so influencer marketing can talk about it. The AI companion is deliberately phase 2.

The product ladder (agreed 26 June, refined 13 July)

StepWhatPhase
1 · Pre-canned reportsWeekly Sportradar roll-ups, built once, near-zero marginal cost — the free tasters that pull users inIN BUILD demoed 13-07
2 · Saved custom reportsUser-built reports from a data-point catalog; column limits are the upsell leverPhase 1

4 · AI companionConversational research agent over the whole datasetPhase 2 · $49.99 tier

Decisions locked

  • Pricing: 99c for the first month (to capture the card), then $14.99/month; the AI tier at $49.99 (Cody, 26 June).
  • In-app payment — Edwin's ruling: accept the store fee of up to 30% rather than bounce users to a web flow; split-test later.
  • Trade hand-off — every team named in a report carries its team ID, so users can trade directly from the research page.
  • Resourcing follows usage metrics: each ladder step is funded by demand at the previous one.

In flight / open

  • The subscription and billing build — Edwin wants it live within weeks; Cody's subscription-packages document (mid-tier ~$39.99 under discussion) is the pricing input.
  • Data-point catalog scope and the custom-report column limit.
  • Report ideas backlog from Cody feeding the pre-canned set.
  • Timing honesty: phase 1 targets "as soon as possible" and may slip past launch by design — it does not gate trading.
  • Related but separate: analyst prices (guest analysts publishing fair-value prices on team pages, Preferred Walk-Ons first) needs its own scoping session and a broker-dealer/advisory separation, per Edwin's 10 July ruling.
