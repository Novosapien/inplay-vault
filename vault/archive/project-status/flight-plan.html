The critical path

Everything below must land in sequence. Trading and the market maker are the two long poles; both Edwin and Troy have stated trading readiness for 22 August is the top priority, ahead of every other request.

  1. Trading execution — complete cancel and cancel-replace, wire fill notifications, finish simulation-replay testing against tZERO QA. Detail →
  2. IPO draft engine — five-day NCAA offering first (simpler), then the NFL round engine with pre-buy book and water line. Detail →
  3. Market maker — quote construction now; then the probability poller, market state, and venue sync to tZERO. Quoting live by NCAA secondary open. Detail →
  4. Opening inventory hand-off — the IPO close must publish each team's unsold-share position into the market maker (open item E27).
  5. Live-game switch-on — flip the data platform's live worker from simulation to production feeds before the first game. Detail →
  6. Synthetic market order — the app-side "just buy it" button, required before the first NFL game.
